Data Beyond Space, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Challenge: Flame in Freefall.

All data: NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) repository, Combustion Science, shown as NASA published it. One table on the Habitat page comes from a NASA White Sands Test Facility paper and is marked as such. This site shows which conditions were tested and which were not. It does not predict how fire behaves on the Moon.

Code and plain-language summaries were written with AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic) and reviewed by the team. The extracted attributes, the sorting of NASA's text by fire question and the drawings (this logo, the route map and the habitat schematic) were also made with AI assistance. NASA's own words always appear in quotation marks. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

Photographs, all NASA and in the public domain, none AI-generated: the Moon (Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University), Earth (Apollo 17 crew) and the space station (NASA/Roscosmos, 2018; the black sky around it was made transparent).
